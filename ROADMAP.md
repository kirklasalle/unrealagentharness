## 🧭 Executive Vision & Mission

The **Unreal Agent Harness** is dedicated to pioneering **autonomous, multi-generational level architecture and game engine intelligence**. By uniting 28 years of Unreal Engine procedural geometry, Win32 direct-injection automation, AI bot navigation physics, and frontier multi-modal reasoning models (Google Gemini 2.5, Anthropic Claude 3.7, OpenAI GPT-4o, and local air-gapped runtimes), UAH transforms human concept prompts into fully lit, textured, and playable 3D tournament levels in seconds.
