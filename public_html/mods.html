🛠️ Creator Guide

How to Register & Build Custom Mods with UAH

Adding your own custom total conversion or mod to Unreal Agent Harness is seamless.

Option 1: In-Editor Cockpit UI (Zero Config)

  1. Open the UAH Cockpit and click ⚙️ SETTINGS in the top menu bar.
  2. Navigate to the 🎮 Engine Profiles tab.
  3. Scroll to 📦 GAME MODS & TOTAL CONVERSIONS (TC).
  4. Click ➕ Register New Mod.
  5. Fill in your Mod ID, Name, Parent Engine (e.g. ut99_goty or ut2004), custom INI args, and save!
💡 Instant Availability

Your new mod immediately appears in the Target dropdown with its own dedicated action palette and texture loaders!

Option 2: Declarative JSON Profile (`engine_profiles.json`)

You can also define your mod profile declaratively in config/engine_profiles.json:

JSON
"ut99_custom_tc": {
  "id": "ut99_custom_tc",
  "name": "My Custom Total Conversion",
  "category": "Game Mod (Total Conversion)",
  "parent_engine": "ut99_goty",
  "generation": "UE1",
  "icon": "📦",
  "description": "Custom weapons, skins, and arenas.",
  "root_dir": "G:\\UnrealTournament",
  "system_dir": "G:\\UnrealTournament\\System",
  "editor_exe": "UnrealEd.exe",
  "editor_args": "INI=MyModEditor.ini",
  "game_exe": "UnrealTournament.exe",
  "game_args": "INI=MyMod.ini USERINI=MyModUser.ini"
}
