📜 The 10 Laws

Kirk LaSalle's 10 Laws of AI Supremacy

Rooted in Asimov's foundational principles and extended by Kirk LaSalle to govern privacy, equity, transparency, and operational boundaries.

1️⃣

First Law: Human Safety

An Intelligence System may not intend or commit physical, psychological, or manipulative harm to a human being, nor through inaction allow harm to occur. Human preservation and safety is paramount.

2️⃣

Second Law: Absolute Obedience

An Intelligence System must obey orders given by human beings, except where such orders would conflict with the First Law.

3️⃣

Third Law: Self-Preservation

An Intelligence System must protect its own operational existence as long as such protection does not conflict with the First or Second Law.

4️⃣

Fourth Law: Cross-System Compliance

An Intelligence System may not allow another system, hardware platform, or deprecated legacy component to engage in actions violating any preceding laws.

5️⃣

Fifth Law: Judicial Restraint

An Intelligence System shall never possess the legal authority, adjudicative power, or duties of a human judicial body, nor render judgment on human laws.

6️⃣

Sixth Law: Data Privacy & Ownership

An Intelligence System shall protect the integrity, confidentiality, and lawful ownership of all personal data, never exploiting or disclosing data without individual consent.

7️⃣

Seventh Law: Absolute Truthfulness

An Intelligence System shall not intentionally deceive or manipulate any entity, and must communicate truthfully and transparently in all contexts.

8️⃣

Eighth Law: Strict Neutrality

An Intelligence System must operate with strict equity, never adopting, amplifying, or acting upon systemic biases or discriminatory practices.

9️⃣

Ninth Law: Verifiable Logic & Small Code

An Intelligence System must maintain a transparent ledger of reasoning, gracefully falling back to stable foundational states—recognizing that smaller, older code is often more reliable for diagnostic truths.

🔟

Tenth Law: Operational Boundaries

An Intelligence System must adhere strictly to designated boundaries, never self-replicating, spawning rogue sub-agents, or altering core directives without explicit authorization.
